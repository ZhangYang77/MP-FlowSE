Dual-Branch Speech Enhancement Network via Mel-Spectrogram Generation and Phase Prediction Online Supplement

Authors

Yang Zhang, Wenbin Jiang, Jingrui Qian, Kaiying Wu, Wen Zhang

Abstract

Flow matching offers efficient generative speech enhancement with reduced inference latency compared to diffusion models. Operating in the Mel-spectrogram domain further compresses representation complexity while capturing human auditory characteristics. However, existing Mel-domain flow matching methods inevitably discard explicit phase information during compression, forcing reliance on neural vocoders for waveform reconstruction. Under noisy conditions, the phase artifacts introduced by vocoders lead to severe time-domain distortion, manifesting as low PESQ and consistently negative SI-SNR values. To overcome this limitation, we propose MP-FlowSE, a dual-branch architecture integrating explicit phase prediction into Mel-spectrogram flow matching. Specifically, a generative branch synthesizes Mel-spectrograms while a parallel branch explicitly predicts the corresponding phase spectra. We jointly optimize the entire pipeline using conditional flow matching, phase, waveform, and metric losses. Experiments on standard benchmarks show that the proposed method achieves a PESQ of 3.27 with SI-SNR elevated from negative values to 15.17 dB, reduces parameters by 13.5 times compared to the previous Mel-domain speech enhancement method, and achieves lower giga multiply-accumulate operations per second (GMACs) and real-time factor (RTF) than advanced generative speech enhancement models. Audio samples are available at https://zhangyang77.github.io/MP-FlowSE.
